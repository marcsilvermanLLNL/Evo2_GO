Figure 2. GO-term-level performance increases with GO-term training frequency. Each
point represents one retained true-path GO term with at least one positive human-test
gene; terms are colored by GO namespace. The x-axis shows the number of training genes
annotated to the term in the live 1,342-taxon min-count-12,000 build. The left panel
reports one-vs-rest GO-term F1 at the single global threshold selected by the
gene-averaged human-test threshold sweep (0.525); the right panel reports the best
one-vs-rest F1 for each GO term over the same threshold grid. Black lines show
frequency-binned medians and shaded bands show interquartile ranges. The analysis
includes 2,162 of 2,186 vocabulary terms with human-test support across 18,608 held-out
human genes. Median F1 at the global threshold was 0.029, whereas median per-term
optimal Fmax was 0.179; Spearman correlations with training frequency were rho=0.51 and
rho=0.50, respectively.
