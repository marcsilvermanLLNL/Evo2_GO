Figure 1. Human-test threshold sweep for Evo2-GO function prediction. Predicted GO-term
probabilities from the best checkpoint (training step 75,000) were thresholded over 99
evenly spaced cutoffs from 0.01 to 0.99 and evaluated on the held-out human test set
(18,608 genes; 2,766 retained true-path GO terms). Precision and recall were computed
per gene and averaged across genes; F1 was then computed from the averaged precision and
recall at each threshold. The maximum gene-averaged F1 was 0.507 at threshold 0.510,
with precision 0.568 and recall 0.458. This threshold sweep defines the reported
CAFA-style gene-averaged Fmax and is not a calibrated posterior probability threshold.
