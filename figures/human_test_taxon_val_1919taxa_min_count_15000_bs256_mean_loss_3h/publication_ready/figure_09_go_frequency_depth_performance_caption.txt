Figure 9. GO-term performance versus training frequency colored by GO graph depth. Each
point represents one retained true-path GO term with at least one positive human-test
gene; the x-axis shows the number of training genes annotated to that term and point
color shows ontology depth computed from is_a and part_of links in go-basic.obo. The
left panel reports one-vs-rest GO-term F1 at the single global threshold selected by the
gene-averaged human-test threshold sweep, whereas the right panel reports per-term
optimal Fmax over the same threshold grid. Black curves show frequency-binned medians
and shaded bands show interquartile ranges. The analysis includes 2,728 of 2,766
vocabulary terms with human-test support across 18,608 held-out human genes. Median
global-threshold GO-term F1 was 0.002, and median per-term optimal Fmax was 0.160.
