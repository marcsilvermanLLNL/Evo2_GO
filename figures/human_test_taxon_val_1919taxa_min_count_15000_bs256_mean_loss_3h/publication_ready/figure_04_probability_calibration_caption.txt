Figure 4. Probability calibration of human-test GO predictions. Predicted probabilities
for all pairs of held-out human genes and retained GO terms (18,608 genes and 2,766
terms) were grouped into 20 equal-width score bins. The reliability curve compares each
bin's mean predicted probability with its observed positive-label fraction; circle area
is proportional to the number of gene-GO decisions in the bin. The histogram shows the
same score-bin occupancy on a logarithmic scale. The Brier score was 0.055, expected
calibration error was 0.178, and maximum calibration error was 0.302, indicating that
the discriminative threshold scores should not be interpreted as well-calibrated
posterior probabilities without calibration.
