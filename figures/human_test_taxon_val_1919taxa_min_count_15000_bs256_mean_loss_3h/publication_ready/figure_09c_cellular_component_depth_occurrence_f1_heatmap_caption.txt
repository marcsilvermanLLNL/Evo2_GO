Figure 9C. Cellular component GO-term performance by GO graph depth and binned training
occurrence. Each heatmap cell groups retained true-path GO terms by ontology depth and
by the number of training genes annotated to the term. The left panel shows the median
one-vs-rest GO-term F1 at the single global threshold (0.510); for each GO term, F1 is
computed across the held-out sequences as 2TP/(2TP+FP+FN), treating that term as the
positive class rather than averaging gene-level scores. The right panel shows the median
per-term optimal Fmax, where each GO term is evaluated at its best threshold over the
threshold grid. Empty gray cells contain no retained terms. Sparse cells are labeled n=1
through n=4, where n is the number of GO terms in that depth-by-occurrence bin. The
heatmap includes 426 cellular component terms across 18,608 held-out human genes; the
median occupied cell score was 0.049 for global-threshold F1 and 0.203 for per-term
optimal Fmax. Exact cell medians, occurrence ranges, and term counts are provided in
figure_09_go_depth_occurrence_f1_heatmap_summary.tsv.
