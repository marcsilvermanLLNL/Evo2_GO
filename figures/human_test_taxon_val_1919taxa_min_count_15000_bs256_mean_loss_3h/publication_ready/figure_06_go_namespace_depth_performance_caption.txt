Figure 6. GO-term performance stratified by ontology namespace and graph depth. The top
row compares one-vs-rest GO-term F1 at the single gene-averaged global threshold with
per-term optimal Fmax across biological process, cellular component, and molecular
function terms. The bottom row summarizes the same metrics by GO graph depth within each
namespace; lines show namespace-specific depth-wise medians and shaded bands show
interquartile ranges. GO graph depth was computed from is_a and part_of parent links in
go-basic.obo. The analysis uses retained true-path GO terms with at least one positive
label in the 18,608-gene human test set (2,728 of 2,766 vocabulary terms). Median
per-term optimal Fmax by namespace was BP=0.140, CC=0.186, and MF=0.208, underscoring
that the headline gene-averaged Fmax summarizes a different quantity than one-vs-rest
GO-term performance.
