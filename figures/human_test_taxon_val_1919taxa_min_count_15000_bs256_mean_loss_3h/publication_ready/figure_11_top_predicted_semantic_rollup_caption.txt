Figure 11. Semantic roll-up of the most frequently predicted GO terms. For each GO
namespace, the 60 non-root retained GO terms with graph depth at least 2 and the largest
number of human-test genes predicted positive at the global threshold (0.510) were
mapped to higher-level ancestors at GO graph depth 3. The plotted rectangles show the
largest ancestor-level semantic themes; rectangle area is proportional to the number of
displayed top predicted terms assigned to the theme, not to model accuracy. Text inside
each block lists the ancestor theme and, for larger blocks, representative child terms.
The full long-tail roll-up is retained in the output tables. The analysis used
predictions for 18,608 held-out human genes and includes 60 BP, 60 CC, and 60 MF terms.
Exact top-term membership and roll-up assignments are provided in
figure_11_top_predicted_semantic_rollup_top_terms.tsv and
figure_11_top_predicted_semantic_rollup_theme_summary.tsv.
