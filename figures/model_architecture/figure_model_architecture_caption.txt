Figure. Evo2-GO dual-encoder architecture. Each representative CDS is represented by a
precomputed, mean-pooled 4,096-dimensional activation from layer blocks.28.mlp.l3 of
Evo2-7B; stored FP16 vectors are cast to FP32 at the trainable model boundary. The sequence
encoder applies LayerNorm, a 4,096-to-2,048 linear projection, GELU, 0.1 dropout, a second
LayerNorm, a 2,048-to-1,024 linear projection, 0.1 dropout, and L2 normalization. In
parallel, 3,384 learned 256-dimensional GO node vectors are encoded by a two-layer GCN over
a fixed, symmetrically normalized ontology adjacency containing self-loops and bidirectional
is_a and part_of edges. Each graph layer aggregates with the adjacency; bias-free
projections map 256 to 512 and then 512 to 1,024 dimensions, with LayerNorm and GELU after
the first projection and LayerNorm followed by L2 normalization after the second. The GCN
therefore supplies two-hop ontology context and is recomputed on each training forward pass.
Unit-normalized sequence and GO representations are compared by a scaled dot product,
equivalent to cosine similarity. The learned scale is initialized to 20 and clamped to [1,
100], producing one raw logit per retained GO term; sigmoid converts logits to multilabel
probabilities. GO vectors serve directly as output heads, with no separate classifier matrix
or term-bias layer. Training uses dense true-path binary targets and mean-reduced asymmetric
loss (gamma-negative 4, gamma-positive 0, probability clip 0.05). Backpropagation updates
both encoders, the GO node features, and the logit scale, while the precomputed Evo2
embeddings and normalized GO adjacency remain fixed.
