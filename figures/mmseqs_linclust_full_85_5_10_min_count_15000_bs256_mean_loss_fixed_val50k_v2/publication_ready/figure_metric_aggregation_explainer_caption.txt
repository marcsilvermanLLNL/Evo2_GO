Figure. Gene-level and GO-term-level metrics summarize the same multilabel prediction matrix
along different axes. Panel A is a schematic illustration, not a sample of test
observations. Gene-level CAFA evaluation treats each row as one predicted label set:
precision and recall are calculated for each gene, averaged across genes, combined into F1,
and maximized over one global threshold. On the MMseqs-cluster test set, this produced
Fmax=0.525 at threshold 0.584 (precision=0.537, recall=0.514). GO-term-level evaluation
treats each column as a binary classification task across all genes and then takes the
median across 3,381 terms. The median term F1 was 0.071 at the gene-optimized global
threshold and the median per-term optimal Fmax was 0.220. True-path propagation supplies
many genes with broad, frequent ancestor terms that the model predicts relatively well,
improving row-wise label-set overlap. In contrast, term-wise aggregation weights every GO
term equally; numerous rare or specific terms with few correct predictions therefore lower
the median. The gene-level and term-level results are complementary rather than
contradictory. The reported test threshold sweep is descriptive; an operational threshold
should be selected using validation data.
