Figure 9. GO-term performance by ontology namespace, graph depth, and binned dataset
occurrence. Rows show (A) molecular function, (B) biological process, and (C) cellular
component (MF=849 terms; BP=2,011 terms; CC=520 terms). Each cell groups GO terms by
depth and occurrence and reports the median F1 calculated independently for each term
across all test sequences. The left column uses the global threshold selected by
descriptive test-set Fmax (0.584); the right column shows per-term descriptive test-set
Fmax. Gray cells contain no terms, and n denotes term counts in sparse cells. The
ontology root terms molecular function (MF) (GO:0003674), cellular component (CC)
(GO:0005575), and biological process (BP) (GO:0008150) were excluded from all reported
metrics.
