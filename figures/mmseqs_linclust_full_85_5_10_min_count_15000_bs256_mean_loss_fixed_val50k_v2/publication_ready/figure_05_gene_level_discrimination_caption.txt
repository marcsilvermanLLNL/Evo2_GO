Figure 5. Sequence-level discrimination on the held-out MMseqs-cluster test set. The
empirical cumulative distribution functions summarize per-gene AUROC and average
precision across 3,092,176 sequences using score-ranked GO-term probabilities. The
second panel groups genes by deciles of true retained-label count and reports median F1
at the test-selected global Fmax threshold together with median average precision; the
shaded band shows the interquartile range for F1. Median per-gene AUROC was 0.986,
median per-gene average precision was 0.527, and median F1 at the global threshold was
0.440. The global threshold was selected to maximize gene-averaged F1 using test labels,
so threshold-dependent F1 values are descriptive (oracle) test-set results rather than
performance at a validation-selected operating threshold. Per-gene AUROC and average
precision are score-ranked metrics and do not use that threshold. The ontology root
terms molecular function (MF) (GO:0003674), cellular component (CC) (GO:0005575), and
biological process (BP) (GO:0008150) were excluded from all reported metrics.
