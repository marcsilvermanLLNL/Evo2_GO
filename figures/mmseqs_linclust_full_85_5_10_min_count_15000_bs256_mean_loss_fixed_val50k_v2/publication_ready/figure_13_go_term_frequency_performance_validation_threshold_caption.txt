Figure 13. GO-term-level performance as a function of annotation frequency in the
full cleaned 30,921,763-sequence dataset. Panel A reports one-vs-rest GO-term F1 at
the validation-selected threshold of 0.61, whereas panel B reports descriptive
per-term test-set Fmax; across 3,381 non-root terms, the respective medians were
0.044 and 0.220 and their Spearman correlations with frequency were 0.339 and
0.218. Points denote individual terms colored by namespace, black lines and shaded
bands denote binned medians and interquartile ranges, and the three ontology root
terms were excluded.
