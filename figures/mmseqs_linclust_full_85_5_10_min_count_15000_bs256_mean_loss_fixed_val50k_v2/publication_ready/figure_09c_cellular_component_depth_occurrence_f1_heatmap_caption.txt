Figure 9C. Cellular component GO-term performance by GO graph depth and binned dataset
occurrence. Each heatmap cell groups retained true-path GO terms by ontology depth and
by the number of sequences annotated to the term in the dataset used for vocabulary
construction. The left panel shows the median one-vs-rest GO-term F1 at the single
test-selected global threshold (0.584); for each GO term, F1 is computed across the
held-out sequences
as 2TP/(2TP+FP+FN), treating that term as the positive class rather than averaging
gene-level scores. The right panel shows the median per-term optimal Fmax, where each GO
term is evaluated at its best threshold over the test-set threshold grid. Both panels
are descriptive (oracle) test-set analyses: the global threshold was selected using all
test labels, and each per-term Fmax independently selects that term's best test-set
threshold. Neither represents performance at a validation-selected operating
threshold. Empty gray cells
contain no retained terms. Sparse cells are labeled n=1 through n=4, where n is the
number of GO terms in that depth-by-occurrence bin. The heatmap includes 520 cellular
component terms across 3,092,176 sequences in the held-out MMseqs-cluster test set; the
median occupied cell score was 0.135 for global-threshold F1 and 0.267 for per-term
optimal Fmax. Exact cell medians, occurrence ranges, and term counts are provided in
figure_09_go_depth_occurrence_f1_heatmap_summary.tsv. The ontology root terms molecular
function (MF) (GO:0003674), cellular component (CC) (GO:0005575), and biological process
(BP) (GO:0008150) were excluded from all reported metrics.
