Figure 3. ROC and precision-recall benchmark curves for the held-out MMseqs-cluster test
set. The best checkpoint was evaluated on 3,092,176 sequences and 3,381 retained
non-root true-path GO terms. The ROC panel shows recall as a function of false positive
rate for the same 0.01 to 0.99 threshold grid used for descriptive (oracle) test-set
Fmax; the precision-recall panel shows the corresponding precision-recall tradeoff.
Gene-averaged curves first compute each quantity per gene and then average across genes,
whereas micro curves pool gene-GO decisions before computing each quantity.
Threshold-grid AUROC was 0.976 gene-averaged and 0.972 micro; threshold-grid AUPRC was
0.424 gene-averaged and 0.494 micro. Fmax maximizes gene-averaged F1 over this test-set
grid and should not be interpreted as performance at a validation-selected operating
threshold. The ontology root terms molecular function (MF) (GO:0003674), cellular
component (CC) (GO:0005575), and biological process (BP) (GO:0008150) were excluded from
all reported metrics.
